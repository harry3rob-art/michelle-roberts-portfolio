Workforce Training · B2B

TECHSIS

Fiber optic career training · Texas · September 2026

The problem

The founder of TECHSIS brings 35 years of military telecom and fiber engineering to a one-week broadband bootcamp, but a workforce board reading his website couldn't tell whether TECHSIS was a training school, a sales company or a grant partner.

The strategy

In my session with the founder we found the buyer who signs the check, the workforce development board, and rebuilt the Light NU message around the first question that buyer asks: are participants still working 90 days later? That one shift carried through the new homepage, the student page and the pricing options, and our SnapGist scan moved his homepage from three out of 10 for clarity to eight.

Youth Nonprofit

Sincerra Kids

A Courtney Wilson Enterprises nonprofit · Western New York · October 2026

The problem

Sincerra Kids' founder started the nonprofit so talented Western New York kids don't have to quit sports and the arts over fees, but her website spoke only to donors, so the families she started it for had no way to apply.

The strategy

I split the brand into two paths, so families who need help paying meet a homepage written for them and the companies and donors who fund each season get their own giving page. The new headline names the exact fees kids are quitting over, so a parent knows in one line whether Sincerra Kids can help.
